Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexyra handles information when you use our writing-enhancement application. Lexyra helps you improve the clarity, tone, and structure of text you provide.

In short: Lexyra processes the text you submit to generate a rewritten version. Your text is sent to the services needed to operate the application, including our AI inference provider. Please do not submit sensitive or confidential information.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Lexyra, the following information may be processed:

  • Text you submit: The writing you enter and the writing style you select, such as professional, concise, confident, recruiter-friendly, or academic.
  • Generated text: The rewritten version returned by the AI service.
  • Technical information: Information that may be included in routine infrastructure or security logs, such as request metadata, timestamps, and error information, depending on the hosting providers' logging practices.

Lexyra does not currently require an account to use its core writing-enhancement functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

  • Provide the requested writing-enhancement functionality.
  • Send text to the AI provider and return generated results.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain service reliability, and protect the application against misuse.

Text you submit should not be considered confidential merely because you submit it through Lexyra.

3. AI Processing and Service Providers

Lexyra uses Cloudflare Workers AI to process submitted text and generate rewritten output. The text required for a request is transmitted to that provider for processing.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may also process technical information when delivering the application and its API. These providers may handle information under their own privacy policies, service terms, and applicable retention practices.

Review the relevant provider policies before submitting information that is personal, sensitive, or confidential. Lexyra does not control the independent data-handling practices of third-party providers.

4. Storage and Retention

Lexyra is designed to process submitted text to fulfill your request and does not currently use a dedicated application database to store writing submissions.

However, this does not mean that every copy of a request is immediately erased. Hosting infrastructure, security logs, backups, or AI providers may retain certain information under their applicable policies and configurations. Retention periods depend on those systems and should be confirmed against their current terms.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Lexyra's core writing functionality is not designed to require advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers. However, the hosting platform or any additional analytics or third-party services introduced in the future may use technical storage or similar technologies. This policy should be updated if those services are added.

6. Sharing of Information

Lexyra does not sell submitted writing to advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers as necessary to operate the application, or disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the application and its communications. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should avoid submitting passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, confidential business material, or other sensitive information.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose what text to submit and may stop using Lexyra at any time. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

Requests can be sent to the contact address below. Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law or by applicable provider retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Lexyra is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other legal requirements apply. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. International Processing

Our hosting and AI providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Applicable safeguards and requirements depend on the services used and the relevant laws.

This policy may be updated as Lexyra's features, infrastructure, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
