Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lexyra handles information when you use the Anna-native version of our AI writing-enhancement application. Lexyra helps you improve the clarity, tone, and structure of text you provide while aiming to preserve its original meaning.

In short: Lexyra processes the text you submit to generate an enhanced version. AI processing is performed through Anna's native application runtime and Sampling capability. Anna and its underlying service providers may process information as part of delivering that functionality. Please do not submit passwords, confidential documents, or other sensitive information.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies specifically to the Anna-native version of Lexyra. It describes the information processed through that version and the services involved in its operation.

Other versions of Lexyra, including separately hosted applications or services, may use different infrastructure and may be governed by separate privacy disclosures.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how you use Lexyra, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Submitted text: The writing you enter into Lexyra for enhancement.
  • Selected writing style: The style you choose, such as Professional, Articulate, Concise, Confident, Recruiter-Friendly, or Academic.
  • Generated text: The enhanced version returned by the AI service.
  • Technical and operational information: Information that may be processed by the application runtime or infrastructure, such as request metadata, timestamps, diagnostic information, and error records.

Lexyra's core writing-enhancement functionality does not currently require you to create a separate Lexyra account. This does not necessarily mean that the platform hosting the application does not have its own account or identity information associated with your use of its services.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through Lexyra is used to:

  • Provide the writing-enhancement functionality you request.
  • Transmit the relevant text and instructions for AI processing.
  • Return the generated result to the application.
  • Validate responses and help preserve the meaning of submitted text.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and protect the application against misuse.

Submitted text is processed to fulfill your request. You should not consider it confidential merely because you submit it through Lexyra.

4. AI Processing and Service Providers

The Anna-native version of Lexyra uses Anna's application runtime and native Sampling capability to access AI functionality. When you request an enhancement, the relevant text and instructions are passed through this mechanism so that an AI-generated response can be returned.

This version does not use the standalone application's former Cloudflare Workers AI integration as its primary AI processing path.

Anna and the service providers involved in delivering its platform may process information under their own privacy policies, service terms, technical configurations, and applicable data-handling practices.

Lexyra is an application operating within the Anna platform. We do not independently control all processing, storage, security, or retention practices of the platform or its underlying providers.

You should review the relevant platform policies and terms before submitting personal, sensitive, or confidential information.

5. Storage and Retention

Lexyra is designed to process submitted text to fulfill your enhancement request. The application does not currently operate a dedicated application database for storing writing submissions.

However, this does not mean that every copy of a request is immediately erased after processing. Information may be temporarily or otherwise retained by the application runtime, platform infrastructure, operational systems, security logs, or underlying AI service providers in accordance with their applicable policies and configurations.

We do not make an independent guarantee about specific retention periods for information processed by Anna or its underlying providers. Those practices are governed by the relevant services and their applicable terms.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Lexyra's core writing-enhancement functionality is not designed to require advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers.

However, the platform hosting Lexyra or other services involved in delivering the application may use technical storage, session mechanisms, or similar technologies necessary for functionality, security, and service operation.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

Lexyra does not sell submitted writing to advertisers.

Information may be processed by Anna and other relevant service providers as necessary to operate the application, deliver AI functionality, maintain infrastructure, diagnose technical issues, and protect the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the application and its users.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken within the scope of our control to protect the application and its communications. However, no online service or electronic transmission can guarantee absolute security.

You should avoid submitting passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, confidential business material, private correspondence, or other information that should not be shared with an AI processing service.

You control the text you choose to submit and may stop using Lexyra at any time.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

Privacy-related questions or requests can be sent to the contact address below. Requests concerning information held by Anna or another service provider may need to be handled under that provider's own privacy procedures.
